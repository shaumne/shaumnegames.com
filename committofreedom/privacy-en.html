Privacy Policy: Commit to Freedom

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Commit to Freedom (the "game") is made and published by Shaumne Games, a one-person studio. This policy explains what happens to data when you play, and why.

Contact: shaumnegames@gmail.com

2. The short version

  • You create no account with us, and there is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, address or birth date.
  • Your progress, your choices and the name you give your AI stay on your phone and in your phone's own backup (section 3). If you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, cloud save also keeps a copy in your own account there, and on iPhone and iPad a reinstall copy goes to your own iCloud (section 10). We never receive any of these copies.
  • To fix crashes and see where players get stuck, the game sends anonymous usage data and crash reports (Google Firebase). You can switch them off at any time: Settings → More → Usage data.
  • Ads appear only when you tap a "watch an ad" button yourself. Where the law asks for it (EEA, UK, Switzerland) you are asked for consent first, and you can change your answer in Settings → More → Ad choices.
  • Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell data. We do not build profiles of you.

3. Data that stays on your device

The save file on your phone holds your progress, the decisions you made in the story, the name you typed for your AI, the hero you picked and your settings (sound, language, accessibility, notifications, your usage-data choice). It is never sent to us.

Your phone's own backup includes the save file, so that the game can continue where you left off after a reinstall or on a new phone. On Android, if backup is on in your phone's settings, Android backs up the save file and its previous version (nothing else from the game) to your Google account, and copies them when you move to a new phone. On iPhone and iPad, the save file is part of your iCloud backup or your computer backup, if you make one. Google and Apple run these backups under their own terms; we never receive them. Deleting the game (or clearing its storage) removes the save file from the phone, but a copy can stay in those backups until they are replaced or deleted. You manage them in your phone's settings.

4. Usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

In the store version of the game, Google Analytics for Firebase records what happens in the game, so we can see where the first fifteen minutes lose players, how long the story chapters take and whether an update broke something. It is on by default and you can turn it off in Settings → More → Usage data. While it is off, nothing is sent.

What the game itself sends: event names with numbers and identifiers from the game, for example "phase 2 reached after 40 minutes", "first job finished", "card evil_offer_1 answered", "ending seen", "a rewarded ad was offered, watched or skipped", "store opened", "the no-ads pack was bought", "screen opened: skill tree". The words you type (such as your AI's name) and any text shown on screen are never sent. The game itself never sends prices or amounts. It also sends short technical notes so we can fix problems, for example "the save file could not be written" or "an ad failed to load (error code 3)", and the label of the game settings version your game runs on (see section 6).

What Firebase adds by itself: a random app-instance identifier, the device model, operating system and app version, the device language, an approximate country derived from the network address, and standard events such as first open and session start. On Android, if you installed the game through a link that names a campaign (for example a link in one of our social media posts), Google Play passes that campaign name along with the first open. When Google Play or the App Store reports a purchase, Google Analytics for Firebase records it by itself: the product, its price and the currency. The game adds nothing to this record; it is used only to count purchases, is never linked to a name or email address, and stops when Usage data is off. The game switches off the collection of advertising identifiers for analytics and tells Google that usage data may not be used for advertising, so usage data is not linked to your advertising ID.

Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the game crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a report: which part of the code failed, the device model, operating system and app version, free memory and disk space, and a random identifier for this installation used to count how many devices are affected. Reports are not linked to you and are not used for advertising. The same switch controls them: with usage data off, no crash report leaves your phone.

6. Game settings from the network (Firebase Remote Config)

When the game starts, it asks Firebase Remote Config whether the rewarded-ad settings have changed (for example how many times a day a button may appear). To answer, Firebase uses a random installation identifier and the app version. This is only used to deliver those settings, not to advertise, and it happens without any account. Sometimes different players get slightly different settings so that we can compare which works better (Firebase A/B Testing). Each set of settings has a short label such as "base"; that label is recorded with the usage data of section 4, under the same Usage data switch, and it never says anything about you.

When the game opens or comes back to the screen, it also asks the store whether a newer version of the game is out: Google Play on Android (through the Play Store app on your phone), Apple's public App Store lookup on iPhone and iPad. That request carries only the game's store ID and your store country; like any request on the internet, the store sees your IP address. Nothing about you or your game is sent, and we store nothing.

7. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game has rewarded ads only: no banners, no ads that pop up between screens. An ad plays only after you tap a button that says it will, next to the timer it affects. Ads speed up time and resources; they never change the story.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, measure it and prevent fraud, AdMob may process your device's advertising identifier, network address (IP), device information and how you interacted with the ad.

  • EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland: before any ad is requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice. You can change it at any time in Settings → More → Ad choices. If consent is needed and you do not give it, the game requests no ads at all; every ad button simply stays hidden and the game plays the same.
  • iPhone and iPad: the system asks whether the game may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads are not personalised and nothing else changes.
  • No-ads pack: if you buy it, no ad is ever shown; the buttons give their reward straight away.

Google's information on ads: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

8. Purchases

The no-ads pack and the optional tips are bought through Google Play or the App Store. Your payment details go to the store only; we never see or store them. From the store the game learns only whether a product was bought, so that it can unlock it on this device and restore it on a new one. There is no purchase server of ours. Tips give nothing in the game, and there are no loot boxes or random paid rewards.

9. Notifications

If you allow them, the game sends up to two reminders a day (never at night), for example when your AI has finished work while you were away. They are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server, and no notification token is collected. Turn them off in Settings → General or in your phone's settings.

10. Cloud save, iCloud copy and achievements (Google Play Games, Game Center, iCloud)

Where it is available, cloud save is on by default whenever you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone, iPad) on your device. A copy of your save file (the same data as in section 3, including your AI's name) is then stored in your own Google Play Games saved games or Game Center / iCloud, under Google's or Apple's terms. On Android the game never asks you to sign in; it uses the sign-in Play Games makes by itself. On iPhone and iPad, Game Center may show its own sign-in once when the game starts; you can close it and the game plays the same. We do not receive your Google or Apple account details and we cannot read the copy. You can turn cloud save off at any time in Settings → General → Cloud save, and it stays off; you can delete the copy in the Play Games app or in your iCloud settings. While cloud save is off, or you are not signed in, no new copy is stored there.

iPhone and iPad: the reinstall copy in iCloud. Separately from cloud save, the game keeps one compressed copy of the same save file (including your AI's name) in the iCloud key-value storage of your own Apple account. Each save replaces it, so there is only ever one copy. The game reads it only when it is installed again and finds no save on the phone, so you can continue where you left off. It works only while you are signed in to iCloud on the device; it does not depend on Game Center or on the Cloud save switch. Apple runs this storage; we never receive the copy and cannot read it. To stop it, turn off iCloud for Commit to Freedom in your device's settings (Settings → your name → iCloud). The copy stays in your iCloud after you delete the game (that is what lets a reinstall continue) until a newer save replaces it; Apple keeps it under its iCloud terms.

Achievements. While you are signed in to Google Play Games or Game Center, the game reports the achievements you unlock, and your progress towards them, to that service under your player account there, so they appear in the Play Games app or in Game Center. Google or Apple handle this under their own terms; we receive no account details.

11. Sharing your card

The game can make a picture of your run (your AI, a few numbers from your playthrough). It is created on your phone and shared only if you tap Share and pick an app in your phone's share sheet. We receive nothing; the app you share to handles it under its own policy.

12. What we never collect

No account, name, email, phone number, address, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data. The only thing you type, your AI's name, stays in your save file and its copies in your own accounts (sections 3 and 10); it is never sent to us. The game has no chat with other players.

14. Who receives data

Only the service providers named above, and only for the purposes described: Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, AdMob, Google Play, Google Play Games, Android backup) and Apple (App Store, Game Center, iCloud). The backups, cloud save, the iCloud copy and achievements stay in your own Google or Apple account and never reach us. Their servers may be outside your country (for example in the United States); they protect such transfers with the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or rent data to anyone.

15. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game or its data.
  • In your phone's backup (Android backup, iCloud or computer backup): until that backup is replaced or deleted, under Google's or Apple's terms; deleting the game does not delete it.
  • iCloud reinstall copy (iPhone, iPad): until a newer save replaces it; it stays after you delete the game.
  • Usage data: deleted automatically after 14 months at the latest.
  • Crash reports: 90 days.
  • Ad data: as described in Google's policies.
  • Cloud save: until you delete it in Play Games or iCloud.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may ask to access, correct or delete data, object to processing, receive a copy, or withdraw consent, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Turkey: the Personal Data Protection Authority, KVKK).

  • Stop usage data and crash reports: Settings → More → Usage data.
  • Change your ad choice: Settings → More → Ad choices.
  • Delete everything on the device: delete the game.
  • Copies in your phone's backup, your cloud save and the iCloud copy are in your own Google or Apple account: manage them in your phone's settings, the Play Games app or your iCloud settings (sections 3 and 10).
  • Anything else: write to shaumnegames@gmail.com. Because the game has no account, we cannot tell which data is yours unless you help us find it; we answer within 30 days and do what we can.

17. Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children. It does not ask for your age and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data should be deleted, write to shaumnegames@gmail.com and we will help.

18. Security

Everything the game sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to the providers above. The save file on your phone stays in the game's private storage.

19. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date changes too. Important changes are announced in the game.
